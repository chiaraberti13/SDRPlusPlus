## 🇬🇧 English

For vulnerabilities specific to this fork or its RFNM changes, use [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/chiaraberti13/SDRPlusPlus/security/advisories/new). For issues affecting the unmodified upstream project, follow the reporting guidance of [AlexandreRouma/SDRPlusPlus](https://github.com/AlexandreRouma/SDRPlusPlus).

Include commit, platform, hardware, reproducible steps and sanitized logs. Do not disclose unpatched vulnerabilities publicly.

## 🇮🇹 Italiano

Per vulnerabilità specifiche di questo fork o delle modifiche RFNM utilizza [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/chiaraberti13/SDRPlusPlus/security/advisories/new). Per problemi che riguardano il progetto upstream non modificato, segui le indicazioni di [AlexandreRouma/SDRPlusPlus](https://github.com/AlexandreRouma/SDRPlusPlus).

Indica commit, piattaforma, hardware, passaggi riproducibili e log privati di dati sensibili. Non divulgare pubblicamente vulnerabilità non ancora corrette.
